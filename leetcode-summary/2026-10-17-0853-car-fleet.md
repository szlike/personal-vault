# 853. Car Fleet

**Recorded:** 2026-10-17

**Status:** OPEN

## Pattern

Sort cars by position, walk from the target backward, and compare arrival times with a stack.

## Approach

Sort the cars by their starting position. Walk from the car nearest the target backward toward the start, and compute each car's arrival time at the target. Keep a stack of fleet arrival times. A car joins the fleet ahead of it when its arrival time is less than or equal to the fleet's arrival time; otherwise it starts a new fleet.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n log n), dominated by the sort.
- Extra space: O(n) for the stack.
