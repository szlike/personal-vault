## Leadership story outline

**Problem:** An engineering process or architecture issue affected correctness, maintainability or delivery.

**Decision:** Define what you changed: technical standard, component/API boundary, CI/CD process, review policy or mentoring approach. Name the alternatives and explain the trade-off.

**Influence:** Who needed to agree? How did you obtain buy-in? How did you support less-experienced developers without becoming a bottleneck?

**Outcome:** Deployment frequency, build times, review turnaround, failures or onboarding time **if measured**. Otherwise explain the concrete observable change.

## Code-review checklist

1. Correct behavior, validation and edge cases.
2. Clear data flow and state ownership; prevent broad re-renders.
3. API/type contracts, backward compatibility and error handling.
4. Test coverage for failure cases, observability and deploy/rollback.
5. Readability, operational complexity and future ownership.

## Past mistakes and feedback

- **Observed:** Describing technology choices without explaining how they affected other developers and the release process.
- **Evidence gap:** No actual dated conflict or junior-mentoring anecdote was recovered; prompts are preparation, not facts.
- **Risk:** Title alone does not show Staff influence; show decisions that changed work beyond your own ticket.

## Points to improve

1. Prepare one specific example each of technical disagreement, mentoring and process improvement.
2. Distinguish direct reports, people management and technical leadership accurately.
3. Include an unsuccessful proposal or compromise and explain the learning.
4. Bring evidence: design document, code-review convention or deployment metric where available.
