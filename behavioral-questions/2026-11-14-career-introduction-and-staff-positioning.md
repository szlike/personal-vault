# Career Introduction and Senior/Staff Positioning

**Recorded:** 2026-11-14  
**Practice period:** September–November 2026

## Focus

Build a clear 60–90-second introduction that connects 10+ years of JavaScript/TypeScript and full-stack engineering to production ownership and technical leadership. Target Senior/Staff IC roles without presenting the background as frontend-only.

## Interview-ready outline

> I'm a full-stack software engineer with over ten years of experience building business-critical applications in financial services. I've worked across React, TypeScript, Java/Spring, Node, Kafka, and Elasticsearch. At Citi, I've built data-heavy visualization tools for trading workflows, worked on real-time data pipelines, and taken product and technical ownership of RFQ-related systems. Previously I led a team of around five developers at CIBC. I enjoy solving performance and reliability problems end to end, and I'm looking for a product-engineering role with broader architectural ownership and impact.

Personalize that to the actual job; do not memorise every sentence or imply all projects were personally architected. For frontend-heavy roles, lead with React architecture and widget performance. For backend roles, lead with data ingestion, consistency, migrations, and reliability.

## Facts and evidence to prepare

- Citi VP/C13, since 2020; full-stack and trading-related work.
- Interactive dashboard: React, D3/Highcharts/AgGrid, drag/drop/resize, real-time updates, Kafka and Elasticsearch.
- RFQ product-owner/technical ownership experience.
- Previous team leadership of approximately five developers; migration of Elasticsearch 7 to 8.
- Elasticsearch was described as 100K+ messages **per product** over 10+ products; the time unit and operational metric need verification before interview use.

## Past mistakes and feedback

- **Observed coaching pattern:** Too much implementation detail before stating the project, responsibility and value. This can obscure Senior/Staff-level scope.
- **Observed concern:** You felt the Citi work had relatively few users and limited perceived impact. Do not exaggerate traffic; explain that users are internal traders and the workflows contribute to revenue.
- **Observed career gap:** Strong frontend background; harder to signal Staff backend/platform depth without explicit service boundaries, failure modes and cross-team decisions.

## Points to improve

1. Prepare 30-, 60-, and 90-second versions; the first sentence should identify role, experience, and specialty.
2. For every major claim, supply one concrete technical decision, one business consequence, and one verified metric.
3. Say **what you personally did** versus what the team did; use 'I' for decisions and 'we' for shared delivery.
4. End with why the target role is the next logical step, not a complaint about Citi or its workload.
5. Rehearse aloud until an interviewer can interrupt and ask a question without losing the story.

## Self-test

- What was your most consequential decision in the past two years?
- What part of the system failed before your change, and what did you measure afterward?
- Why is your experience beyond a strong Senior frontend engineer?
