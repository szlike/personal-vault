# Hiring Manager Preparation for eBay Goldin MTS 2

**Recorded:** 2026-11-13

## Focus

Prepare concise STAR stories that demonstrate:

- Measurable impact and ownership
- Leading through influence
- Technical disagreement and conflict resolution
- Decision-making under ambiguity
- Quality, reliability, and operational improvement
- Learning from setbacks
- Motivation for eBay Goldin: building trust, correctness, traceability, and customer confidence for high-value collectibles

## Interview framing

Connect each story to your scope, technical judgment, collaboration, trade-offs, and outcome.
