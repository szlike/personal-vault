# Shopify Intro Call

**Recorded:** 2026-10-05

## The call

This was a 30-minute initial chat with Justin Kim, Shopify Talent Acquisition, on October 5, 2026, from 1:30 to 2:00 PM EDT (Eastern Daylight Time).

## What happened

Robin had NOT read the candidate guide that HR (Human Resources) sent ahead of the call. The guide discussion was skipped, and the silence was awkward.

The guide was later walked through in a browser session. It included a leadership memo, that leaders re-qualify yearly and are about 40 percent better annually, a Reflexive AI usage memo, and the River agent. A thank-you email was drafted for Robin to send.

## Decision

Rejection on October 14, 2026, via Edna Delgado Avila, Shopify Technical Recruitment Lead. Edna had taken over Robin's process on October 9 after Justin Kim's departure. The thread is closed.

## Past mistakes & points to improve

- The standing rule from this call: proactively flag HR-sent preparation materials and checklists the day before an interview, not the day of. The guide arrived before the call, and nobody checked whether it had been read. A day-before check would have caught it.
- Never walk into an intro call without reading what HR sent. The guide is part of the interview, even when the call itself is only 30 minutes.
