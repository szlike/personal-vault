## The answer

The answer was drafted through 3 rounds of making it shorter. The final version is one paragraph.

AI writes about 80 percent of the code under his direction, while he reviews and guides at the architect level. The concrete example is Kafka-to-Elasticsearch consumer scaffolding: the repetitive consumer code is generated, and he owns the design, the review, and the correctness. He avoids AI for under-specified design decisions, where the problem itself is not yet clear enough for a tool to help.
