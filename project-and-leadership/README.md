# Project & Leadership Experience

## MTS 2 / architect-round framing

Use project stories to demonstrate architecture, technical leadership, risk management, and durable operational outcomes.

For eBay Goldin, relate design choices to a high-value collectibles marketplace:

- Auction bidding and real-time updates
- Inventory and order lifecycle for unique physical items
- Authentication, provenance, grading, and chain of custody
- Search and discovery
- Payments, payouts, disputes, and fraud/risk controls
- Strong audit trails, idempotency, reliability, and clear customer communication

## Story template

For each project, capture:

1. Context and business/customer problem
2. Your role, scope, and constraints
3. Technical design and trade-offs
4. Leadership and cross-team influence
5. Outcome, metrics, and lessons learned

> Add one Markdown file per project once you provide its details. Personal experience has deliberately not been inferred or invented.
